🔒 Open your private log

Multi-user · encrypted · stays on this device. Each person uses their own passcode to get their own private, separate log.

First time? Just pick a name + a passcode and hit Open my log — it creates your private vault.

Returning? Enter the same passcode to unlock your existing notes.

⚠️ Your passcode is the only key. If you forget it, your notes cannot be recovered (there is no reset — nothing is stored on a server). Different passcode = different user = different log.
