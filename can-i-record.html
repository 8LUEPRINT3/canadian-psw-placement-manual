🎙️ Can I Record This?

📋 My Log 📎 Evidence Guide GitHub
Educational guidance, not legal advice. This page summarizes general principles for PSW students in Ontario/Canada. Workplace policy, your college's placement agreement, and PHIPA always take priority. When in doubt, ask your clinical instructor before you record anything.

The two questions that matter

When you wonder "can I record this?", you're really asking two different questions — and they often have different answers:

QuestionWhat it's aboutWho answers it
1. Is it legal?Criminal law — can I be charged?The Criminal Code of Canada
2. Is it allowed at work?Privacy law + workplace policy — can I be disciplined/fired?PHIPA, your employer, your placement agreement

The key point: something can be *legal* under the Criminal Code yet still get you removed from placement for violating PHIPA or your site's policy.

Recording conversations (audio) — Canada

Canada is a "one-party consent" jurisdiction for private communications. Under s.184 of the Criminal Code, it's generally not a crime to record a private conversation that you are part of, even without telling the other person.

Caution That's about criminal law only. It does not mean recording is acceptable at your placement site.

  • You generally cannot legally record a conversation you're not part of (e.g. hiding a device and recording two other people).
  • Recording a client, their family, or a colleague on a personal device is almost always against workplace policy, even if not a crime.

Photos, video, and audio of clients = PHI

Under PHIPA (Personal Health Information Protection Act), a photo, video, or recording of a client — or even their name, room number, or face visible in the background — is Personal Health Information.

Never photograph, film, or record a client on your personal device. This is the same violation as the "social-media selfie" scenario in the manual — it's a PHIPA breach and can end your placement and your career.

Setting-by-setting reality for PSWs

SettingRecording reality
LTC / retirement home / hospitalAlmost universally prohibited on personal devices. Institutional policy + PHIPA govern.
Home care / communityYou're inside a client's private home — recording raises additional privacy/consent issues. Don't do it without explicit direction.
AnywhereOnly record if explicitly directed by your employer/nurse for a legitimate purpose, on an approved device, per written policy. Rare for a PSW.

What a PSW should do instead

Your real evidence tool is documentation, not a recording device.
  1. Write it down — a factual, dated, timed DARP note in the official chart (or your private log).
  2. Report it — tell the RN/RPN or supervisor immediately, verbally and in writing.
  3. Use the official process — incident reports, EASI screening, and the mandatory-reporting lines (LTC 1-866-434-0144, RHRA 1-800-361-7254, OPG&T 1-800-366-0335).
  4. Preserve your record — keep a timestamped copy (your log's "receipt" feature) so there's a clear, factual account.

A complete, timely written note is more useful and more defensible than any secret recording — and it won't get you kicked out of placement.
