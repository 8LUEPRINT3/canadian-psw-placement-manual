# 🎮 PSW Placement Simulator

> **The practice engine.** This is the part to use every day — it turns the manual's knowledge into clinical judgment.

---

## How it works

Ask your coach to **"Start Simulator"** and it presents **one unscripted clinical dilemma at a time**. You respond in your own words (what you'd say, what you'd do, what you'd chart). You are then scored **out of 30** across three domains (10 each).

**Three scored domains:**

1. **Communication & Empathy** — active listening, validation, no defensiveness, eye level, dignity.
2. **Scope & Safety Adherence** — stays in UCP scope, declines out-of-scope tasks, respects client rights, flags abuse-reporting triggers.
3. **Documentation & Reporting** — complete factual DARP note; correct verbal targets (titles, OPG&T, Ministry numbers).

**Rubric (out of 30):**

| Band | Score | What it means |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Excellent | 9–10 | Full empathy, correct scope boundaries, flawless DARP and reporting targets |
| Proficient | 7–8 | Correct but vague/defensive wording, hesitation, or a minor DARP gap |
| Developing | 5–6 | Dismissive/aggressive tone, folds under pressure on scope, opinions instead of facts |
| Critical failure | 0–4 | No empathy, performs a controlled act or violates rights, fails to document/report |

---

## Simulator ground rules

1. **One dilemma at a time** — the coach presents one dilemma, then **STOPS and waits** for your input.
2. **Complexity & tension** — expect competing pressures: a busy nurse pushing out-of-scope tasks, an angry family member demanding private info, responsive behaviors, or suspected abuse with high emotional stakes.
3. **Three-domain scoring** — as above.

---

## How to answer (your checklist)

Use the **4-step clinical loop** (Part D of the manual):

1. **Acknowledge & Validate** — eye level, listen, reflect feelings, never argue.
2. **Stay in Scope (No Diagnosis)** — observe with your senses; never name a cause.
3. **Document Factual Data (DARP)** — objective facts + direct quotes.
4. **Escalate & Report** — tell the RN/RPN immediately; never leave a client in distress alone.

Always end with a **DARP note**: **D** (data) → **A** (action) → **R** (response) → **P** (plan).

## Example scenarios you'll face

The simulator draws from the **10 critical scenarios** in Part G of the manual:

1. Client care refusal (dignity & autonomy)
2. Suspected physical abuse in LTC (mandatory reporting)
3. Suspected financial abuse in retirement home
4. Suspected neglect in home care
5. High temperature (vitals deviation)
6. Dyspnea / respiratory distress (emergency)
7. Severe pain in a non-verbal client (5th vital sign)
8. Family demands medical details (PHIPA)
9. Asked to do out-of-scope dressing & medication
10. Break-room gossip & social-media selfie (ethics & privacy)
